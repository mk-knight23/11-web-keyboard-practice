What Is a Good Typing Speed?

The average typing speed is roughly 40 words per minute; among people who take online typing tests it's about 52 WPM. 60–80 WPM is good, and 100+ is professional territory.

Typing speed benchmarks by level

WPM uses a standard convention: one "word" equals five characters, so scores are comparable across tests and languages. Where you land:

LevelTypical WPMWhat it means in practice
Beginner / hunt-and-peck10–25Eyes on the keyboard; typing is the bottleneck for everything
Average~40Functional for everyday email and documents
Good60–80Comfortably above average; typing rarely slows you down
Fast80–100Professional level; thoughts flow to the screen
Competitive100–150+Top tier of typing-test takers; contest territory

Sources: a 2018 study of 136 million keystrokes from 168,000 volunteers on an online typing test measured an average of ~52 WPM (Dhakal, Feit, Kristensson & Oulasvirta, CHI 2018); earlier lab research on everyday computer users found ~40 WPM for transcription and ~33 WPM when composing text (Karat et al., CHI 1999). Online-test samples skew toward people who practice, which is why the two averages differ.

Typical speeds by profession

Commonly cited working ranges from industry surveys — treat them as rough guidance rather than job requirements; individual speeds vary widely with experience and practice:

RoleTypical WPM
Office worker (general)~40
Data entry clerk50–60
Software developer60–70 (lower on symbol-heavy code)
Writer / journalist70–80
Transcriptionist80–100
Competitive typist120+

Developers reading their 60–70 with suspicion: code WPM genuinely runs 30–50% below prose because of symbols and shifted characters — see code typing practice for why, and how to train it.

Speed is only half the number

A WPM score without its accuracy is close to meaningless: 80 WPM at 90% accuracy produces slower, messier real-world output than 65 WPM at 98%. When you benchmark yourself, record both numbers — and if the gap between your raw and net WPM is large, work through the typing accuracy test before chasing speed. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average typing speed?
Roughly 40 words per minute for the general population. A large 2018 study of 168,000 online typing test takers measured an average of about 52 WPM — a sample that skews toward people who practice.
What is a good typing speed?
60–80 WPM is a good typing speed — comfortably above average and fast enough that typing stops being the bottleneck for most office and writing work. 80–100+ WPM is professional level.
Is 100 WPM fast?
Yes. 100 WPM is well above average and at the level expected of professional typists. Sustaining it with high accuracy puts you in the fastest group of typing test takers.
How do I find out my typing speed?
Take a timed typing test — 60 seconds is the standard benchmark length. Repeat it a few times on different days; your true speed is the trend, not a single run.
